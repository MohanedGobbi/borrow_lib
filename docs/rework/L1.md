## Rules for the seal (write these into DESIGN.md and its sidecar)
- **Official colours.** The seal keeps its colours. It is the one deliberate exception to the
  green, white and red palette, as any university crest is. Never recolour, outline, add a shadow
  to, stretch or crop it.
- **Clear space:** at least 25% of its height on every side.
- **Size:** at least 28px, at most about 72px (the source resolution).
- **Grounds:** white, surface grey, or the dark hero photo under its scrim. Never on red, and never
  on green fills.
- **Next to the wordmark,** separated by a hairline divider: the seal comes first in reading order
  (left in LTR, right in RTL), then "LibBorrow", then the university line. The seal never replaces
  the wordmark.
