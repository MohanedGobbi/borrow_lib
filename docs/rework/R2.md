## How "calm" should read
- **Same identity:** the serif display face for page titles (already inherited) and large figures;
  Public Sans for everything operational; tabular figures; hairlines; generous but denser spacing
  than the landing.
- **No photography, no motion libraries and no scroll effects inside the app.** Only quick,
  purposeful transitions: 150–200 ms, transform and opacity only.
- **Staff desk:** a refined work surface in the spirit of Apple's productivity apps and the best
  university admin systems.
  - The sidebar and header must feel part of the new world: the wordmark, a quiet surface and a
    clear active state.
  - Tables: excellent alignment, restrained row hover, and an action column that doesn't shout.
  - The Overview vitals and Analytics figures use the serif display face for large numbers.
- **Library card on Profile:** the one object students carry, so make it beautiful. A real card
  with the wordmark, the QR code, the student's name and ID, and a hairline frame in university
  green. **No red** (red means late).
- **My loans:** keep its information and states (countdowns, renew, withdraw, show card), restyled
  so the most important loan, a ready hold or the soonest due, reads first.
- **Dialogs and toasts:** consistent with the new type and spacing.
- **Colour:** green, white and red only. Red only for late, rejected and destructive.
