# Surface brief: LibBorrow web app (all routes)

Scope: whole web app; public side (landing, catalog, book, sign-in/up) carries the identity at full strength, signed-in and staff screens share it but stay quiet and fast.
Mode: Persuade for landing; Operate everywhere else.
Audience: students on phones between classes; circulation-desk staff on desktops. EN / FR / AR (RTL).
Owner constraints: green, white and red only (red = late/destructive); serious university tone; not bulky, not template-like, not slow on phones; wordmark-only logo; no 3D. Photos are admin-editable placeholders (Unsplash, credited).
Quality bar: sit beside Apple, top university sites (Harvard, Oxford, MIT) and modern library sites (NYPL, British Library).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The university-library portal everyone expects, finished to Apple and Oxford standard; refuses the SaaS-template look, card grids, gradients and gimmicks.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, one deep university green, red only for late or destructive. Black-and-white architectural photography. A serif display face for headlines and the wordmark; Public Sans for UI; tabular figures; hairlines; generous space.

STORY: A student sees a serious library, finds a book in one search, and knows exactly when to collect and return it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed monochrome library photograph under a quiet translucent header; wordmark left; a large serif headline and one wide search field over the image's calm area; Sign in as the only other action.

FORM: canon (standing exit), seed a11b28e8, code-led; Lenis and GSAP on public pages only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
