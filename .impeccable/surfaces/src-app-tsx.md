# Surface brief: LibBorrow web app (all routes)

Scope: whole web app — landing, auth, catalog, book detail, cart, my requests, profile/ID card, scan lookup, staff dashboard (overview, approvals, pickups, loans, inventory, reservations, users, analytics, activity).
Mode: Operate (landing is a light Persuade surface inside the same world).
Audience: students (phones, between classes) and circulation-desk staff (desktop, office light). FR / AR (RTL) / EN.
Quality bar: sit alongside library discovery systems (Primo, WorldCat) and Linear / GitHub.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, executed impeccably — a serious university-library portal. Refuses nothing clever; refuses sloppiness, decoration and playfulness.

OWN-WORLD: White pages, one deep university green for primary actions and the header mark, red only for overdue, rejection and destructive actions. Public Sans with Noto Sans Arabic, tabular figures, 1px hairline borders, 6px radius, quiet shadows, lucide icons.

STORY: A student finds a book, sees if a copy is on the shelf, requests it, and always knows the pickup deadline and due date. Staff clear queues fast.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing: search-first — headline plus a large catalog search field and the borrowing rules as four numbered steps. Staff: left sidebar, fixed row of vital counts, work queue below.

FORM: canon (standing exit), seed e298055f, code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
